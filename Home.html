Finger Bow-Bell

The Finger Bow-Bell is a conference held by the Five Fingers of the Backstreets, where they discuss and strategize to maintain their control over the Backstreets. This secretive meeting is a key aspect of their power and influence, allowing them to coordinate their actions and resolve disputes among themselves. The Finger's meeting are a rare opportunity for them to come together and plan their next moves, ensuring they can keep the Backstreets under their thumb and prevent any inter Ference from other Syndicates or higher authorities.

The Five Fingers

The Five Fingers, also known as the Fingers of the Backstreets or the Hand, is a term used to refer to the “Star of the City” Syndicates in the City. They control the Backstreets and are among the major factions in the universe of Project Moon.
