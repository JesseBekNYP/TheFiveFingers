FingerBowBell

The Finger Bow-Bell is a conference held by the Five Fingers(highlight) of the Backstreets, where they discuss and strategize to maintain their control over the Backstreets. This secretive meeting is a key aspect of their power and influence, allowing them to coordinate their actions and resolve disputes among themselves. The Finger's meeting are a rare opportunity for them to come together and plan their next moves, ensuring they can keep the Backstreets under their thumb and prevent any inter Ference from other Syndicates or higher authorities.
